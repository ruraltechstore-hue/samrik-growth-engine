# Expand the About Us Page

## Goal
Transform the existing About Us page into a detailed, professional company profile while preserving the current Samrik Solutions design system, navigation, footer, and responsive behavior.

## Implementation
- Review the current About page and shared section, card, icon, button, and FAQ patterns.
- Expand the page in the requested narrative order: hero, company overview, services, mission, vision, approach, values, audiences, industries, differentiators, service philosophy, partnership invitation, FAQs, and final call to action.
- Preserve any valid existing About content by integrating it where it supports the new structure; avoid unsupported claims and invented company facts.
- Link service calls to the existing service pages and partnership/contact calls to their existing pages.
- Keep the page maintainable by defining repeated card and process content as structured page data and rendering it through shared visual patterns.
- Retain or update the About route metadata with a unique title, description, Open Graph fields, and social card metadata.

## Verification
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for readable cards, headings, buttons, and FAQ interactions.
- Confirm all internal links open the intended existing pages and no new navigation item is introduced.
- Confirm the supplied copy remains under the correct section and no prohibited claims were added.
- Confirm the project compiles successfully.
