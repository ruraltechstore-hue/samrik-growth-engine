# Build a Professional Corporate Website for "Samrik Solutions"

Create a modern, professional, responsive corporate website for a company called **Samrik Solutions**.

## Company Overview

**Samrik Solutions** is a sales and business-development solutions company specializing in:

1. SaaS Sales

2. Logistics Sales

3. Educational Sales

The company helps businesses generate leads, acquire customers, expand their sales reach, and build long-term business relationships.

The website should communicate:

* Professionalism

* Trust

* Business growth

* Sales expertise

* Strong customer relationships

* Modern technology

* Reliable business partnerships

The website should be suitable for startups, SaaS companies, logistics companies, educational institutions, training organizations, and other businesses looking for sales and business-development support.

# IMPORTANT DESIGN INSTRUCTIONS

Do not make the website look like a generic AI-generated landing page.

Make it look like a real professional B2B company website.

Use:

* Strong typography

* Professional spacing

* Consistent cards

* Subtle animations

* Clean sections

* High-quality business illustrations

* Clear CTAs

* Consistent visual hierarchy

Keep the website easy to edit later.

All company information such as email, phone number, address, social links, and logo should be stored in an easily editable configuration/component rather than scattered throughout the code.

Do not add fake customer testimonials, fake statistics, fake client logos, or claims about revenue/customers unless they are provided later.

Use placeholder content where actual company information has not yet been provided.
